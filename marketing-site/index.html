Questions

Before you join.

Do I need to know anyone to join?

No. Most people join a team of strangers. You’ll be placed with a squad that’s actively recruiting, and you can bring people later if you want to.

What if I’m out of shape?

Everyone starts at base camp. Zero Day counts any cardio — a walk counts — plus air squats, lunges and push ups, at whatever number you can do today. The point is showing up, not the number.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now, and Zero Day stays free for good. Soon, camera-verified competitions will be paid — you pay for a fair competition where every rep is counted on camera, not for a subscription. Founding members and founding crews who join before paid competitions launch are permanently exempt. No catch.

How big is a team?

Small on purpose. A team can have up to 10 Musters, and most competitions cap it at 5 — so every member counts and nobody can hide. A bigger crew — a shift, a box, a run club — is split into squads that all climb the same map.

I lead a crew. How much work is it for me?

Almost none. I handle the setup — your only job is getting your crew to download the app. Tell us about your crew and we’ll come back with a matchup and a start date.

What happens if I miss a few days?

Nothing bad. Your team keeps going and you pick back up. Coming back doesn’t require an explanation.

What do you do with my data?

Your activity is visible to your teammates and the other people in your competition, and nowhere else. We never sell it or use it to market to you. Apple Health data is used only for app features — logging your activities, your readiness score and your stats — never for advertising.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. Android teammates use the web version at app.joinmuster.com today — same squads, same map, same team chat — so a mixed iPhone and Android crew can still compete together. The Android app is in the works.
