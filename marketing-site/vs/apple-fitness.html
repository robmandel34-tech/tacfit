Compare · Muster Up vs Apple Fitness+

Fitness+ gives you the workout.
Muster Up gives you a team.

Apple Fitness+ is a subscription library of guided workouts. Muster Up is a free team fitness competition — small squads, two weeks, a shared map. One supplies the class; the other supplies the people who expect you to show up for it.

Apple Fitness+ prices are from apple.com as of September 2026 and may change. Muster Up pricing is described on this site’s home page.

Pick Apple Fitness+ if…

  • You want coached, follow-along workouts you can start in one tap.
  • You already show up consistently and want variety and good programming.
  • You live in the Apple ecosystem and like closing rings.

Pick Muster Up if…

  • You have quit a workout plan before because nobody noticed when you stopped.
  • You want a team small enough that your workout actually matters to someone else.
  • Your crew is split between iPhone and Android — the web version keeps everyone on one squad.
  • You lead a shift, a gym or a run club and want a real scoreboard without running it yourself.

Take the class. Bring the squad.

A Fitness+ session recorded to Apple Health can count toward your team’s climb, so the two stack cleanly: Apple coaches the workout, your squad makes sure it happens.

Questions people ask

Is Muster Up an Apple Fitness+ alternative?

They do different jobs. Fitness+ gives you the workout; Muster Up gives you a squad that expects you to do it. Many people use both, and Muster Up is free.

Do Apple Fitness+ workouts count in Muster Up?

Muster Up reads cardio from Apple Health. When a Fitness+ workout is recorded to Apple Health — cardio classes in particular — it can count toward your squad. Strength and yoga count in competitions that include those activity types.

Do I need an Apple Watch for Muster Up?

No. Your phone is enough. An Apple Watch helps because it records cardio to Apple Health automatically, but you can also log activities directly in the app.

What does Muster Up cost?

Nothing right now, and Zero Day stays free for good. Camera-verified competitions will be paid later — you pay for a fair competition where every rep is counted on camera, not for a subscription. Founding members and founding crews who join before paid competitions launch are permanently exempt.

What if some of my crew are on Android?

They use the web version at app.joinmuster.com today — same squads, same map, same team chat — so a mixed iPhone and Android crew can still compete together. The Android app is in the works.
