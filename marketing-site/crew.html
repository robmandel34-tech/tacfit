What this doesn’t cost you

No money, no contract, no subscription. We don’t sell or share your people’s data, ever. We won’t market to your members outside the app. If it doesn’t serve your crew, you stop and nothing happens.

How much work is it for me?

Almost none. I handle the setup — your only job is getting your crew to download the app.

How big are the teams?

Small on purpose. A team can have up to 10, and most competitions cap it at 5 — every member counts, no one can hide. A crew of 30 becomes six squads of five climbing the same map.

What’s the catch?

There isn’t one. Founding crews join before paid competitions launch and are permanently exempt. Zero Day stays free for good.

A real scoreboard

Every activity your people log moves your crew along the map. Two weeks, one shared climb, a finish line everyone can see.

Squads, not leaderboards

Crews are split into squads small enough that every person counts. Nobody is a rounding error.

Your people’s data stays theirs

Activity stays inside the app and the team. We never sell it, share it, or use it to market to your members.

You pick the activities

Competitions are fully customizable — choose exactly which activities count, with no limit on the types you include. If it’s healthy and your crew wants some inspiration to do more of it, Muster Up can run a competition around it.
