Questions people ask

Do I have to give up my tracker?

No — keep it. Muster Up doesn’t replace a wearable; it adds the team. Your tracker keeps scoring your body, and your squad gives you a reason to show up on the days the score says you’d rather not.

Does Muster Up sync with my tracker?

Muster Up reads workouts from Apple Health. Many tracker apps — WHOOP, Garmin, Strava and others — can share workouts to Apple Health; once that’s switched on, those workouts count toward your squad with no extra logging. If your tracker doesn’t share to Apple Health, or you’re on Android, logging a workout in the app takes a few seconds.

Does Muster Up need a wearable?

No. Your phone is enough. Cardio syncs from Apple Health — which can be fed by an Apple Watch, a tracker app or Strava — and air squats, lunges and push ups are logged in the app.

What does Muster Up cost?

Nothing right now, and Zero Day stays free for good. Camera-verified competitions will be paid later — you pay for a fair competition where every rep is counted on camera, not for a subscription. Founding members and founding crews who join before paid competitions launch are permanently exempt.

How big is a Muster Up team?

Small on purpose. A team can have up to 10 Musters, and most competitions cap it at 5 — so every member counts and nobody can hide. A bigger crew — a shift, a box, a run club — is split into squads that all climb the same map.
