Compare · Muster Up vs WHOOP

WHOOP measures you.
Muster Up gives you a team.

WHOOP is a wearable and a membership built around your own recovery and strain. Muster Up is a free team fitness competition — squads of up to 5, two weeks, a shared map. They aren’t rivals so much as different answers to different problems.

WHOOP prices are from whoop.com support pages as of September 2026 and may change. Muster Up pricing is described on this site’s home page.

Pick WHOOP if…

  • You want continuous biometrics — HRV, sleep stages, recovery — measured by a sensor.
  • You already train hard and want to fine-tune load and rest.
  • You are happy training on your own and just want better data.

Pick Muster Up if…

  • The problem isn’t data, it’s showing up — and nobody notices when you stop.
  • You want a team small enough that your workout actually matters to someone else.
  • You lead a crew — a shift, a box, a run club — and want a real scoreboard without running it yourself.
  • You don’t want to spend $199–$359 a year to get moving.

Plenty of Musters wear a WHOOP.

The band tells you how recovered you are. Your squad is the reason you lace up anyway. If your WHOOP app shares workouts with Apple Health, those workouts can count toward your team — no extra logging.

Questions people ask

Is Muster Up a WHOOP alternative?

Not really — they solve different problems. WHOOP measures your body; Muster Up gives you a team and a reason to show up. Plenty of people would benefit from both, and Muster Up costs nothing.

Does Muster Up sync with WHOOP?

There is no direct WHOOP integration. Muster Up reads cardio from Apple Health, so if you have connected your WHOOP app to Apple Health, workouts that land there can count toward your squad.

Does Muster Up need a wearable?

No. Your phone is enough. Cardio syncs from Apple Health (which can be fed by an Apple Watch, WHOOP, Strava and others), and air squats, lunges and push ups are logged in the app.

What does Muster Up cost?

Nothing right now, and Zero Day stays free for good. Camera-verified competitions will be paid later — you pay for a fair competition where every rep is counted on camera, not for a subscription. Founding members and founding crews who join before paid competitions launch are permanently exempt.

How big is a Muster Up team?

Squads are 5 people at most, so every member counts and nobody can hide. A bigger crew is split into squads that all climb the same map.
